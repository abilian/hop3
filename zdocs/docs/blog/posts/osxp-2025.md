# Hop3 at OSXP 2025

*December 2025*

We presented Hop3 at [OSXP 2025](https://www.opensource-experience.com/) (Open Source Experience) in Paris. The talk "From Self-Hosting Complexity to Production-Ready Sovereignty" explored how Hop3 bridges the gap between easy deployment and true digital independence.

## Talk Summary

The presentation covered:

- **The self-hosting paradox**: Why it's simultaneously easier and harder than ever
- **Production-ready sovereignty**: What it means to truly own your infrastructure
- **Hop3's architecture**: How we achieve simplicity without sacrificing power
- **Real-world deployments**: Case studies and lessons learned
- **The road ahead**: Upcoming features and community roadmap

## About OSXP

Open Source Experience is one of Europe's largest open source conferences, bringing together thousands of developers, enterprises, and open source enthusiasts in Paris each year.
