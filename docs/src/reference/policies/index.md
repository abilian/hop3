# Project Policies and Guidelines

The Hop3 project is built on transparency, security, and open collaboration. The following documents outline the policies and guidelines that govern our community, our development practices, and our legal compliance.

We ask all contributors and community members to read these policies and to help us keep the ecosystem healthy and secure.

### [Code of Conduct](./code-of-conduct.md)

Our Code of Conduct sets the standard for behavior within our community. It states our expectations for a respectful and inclusive environment for everyone involved in the Hop3 project.

### [Security Policy](./security-policy.md)

This document details our internal security practices and explains how to disclose vulnerabilities responsibly. Security researchers and anyone reporting a security issue should read it first.

### [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md)

Our Privacy Policy explains what data we collect through our project services (like our website and repositories) and what data the self-hosted Hop3 software does *not* collect. It also covers how we protect your information and respect your data sovereignty.

### [Open Source Compliance Program](./open-source-compliance.md)

This document describes our program for managing open source licenses, dependencies, and contributions in line with the ISO/IEC 5230 standard. It ensures we meet our legal obligations and keep the software supply chain transparent.

### [Evangelism Guide](./evangelism.md)

This document collects ideas and guidelines for community members who want to advocate for Hop3. It suggests how to promote the project and its values respectfully.
