About Hop3 Marketplace

The App Store for Self-Hosting

Hop3 Marketplace is the public storefront for the Hop3 platform. It's designed to solve the "Self-Hosting Discovery Problem" by transforming a list of technical packages into an attractive, App Store-grade catalog.

Our mission is to prove that self-hosting open-source software is not only viable but superior to SaaS—offering the same ease of discovery with the added benefits of sovereignty and control.

Why Self-Host?

Data Sovereignty: Your data stays on your infrastructure. No vendor lock-in, no surprise price changes, no terms of service updates that compromise your privacy.

Cost Control: Pay for compute, not per-seat licensing. Scale on your own terms without usage-based billing surprises.

Customization: Open-source applications can be modified, extended, and integrated to fit your exact needs.

About Hop3

Hop3 is a modern self-hosting platform that makes deploying and managing open-source applications simple. With a single command, you can deploy complex applications complete with databases, caching, and TLS certificates.
