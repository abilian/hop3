# Moodle

Moodle is the world's most widely used learning management system (LMS), powering online education for schools, universities, corporations, and training organizations worldwide. It provides a flexible platform for creating engaging online learning experiences.

## Key Features

- **Course Management**: Create and organize courses with multiple formats
- **Activity Modules**: Assignments, quizzes, forums, wikis, workshops, and more
- **Gradebook**: Comprehensive grading and progress tracking
- **Competency-Based Learning**: Define and track competencies and learning paths
- **Collaboration Tools**: Forums, messaging, groups, and peer assessment
- **Mobile Learning**: Native mobile apps for iOS and Android
- **Plugin Ecosystem**: Thousands of plugins for extended functionality
- **LTI Integration**: Connect with external learning tools

## Use Cases

- K-12 and higher education institutions
- Corporate training and employee development
- Professional certification programs
- Online course creators and e-learning businesses
- Compliance training delivery
- Blended learning environments

## Why Choose Moodle?

Moodle's open-source nature means unlimited scalability without per-user licensing fees. Its active community ensures continuous improvement and a vast ecosystem of plugins and integrations.
