# Matomo

Matomo (formerly Piwik) is the leading open-source web analytics platform, providing a privacy-respecting alternative to Google Analytics. It gives you full ownership of your analytics data while offering comprehensive insights into your website traffic.

## Key Features

- **Complete Analytics**: Page views, visits, referrers, goals, e-commerce tracking
- **Real-Time Data**: See visitor activity as it happens
- **Privacy Compliant**: GDPR, CCPA, and PECR ready out of the box
- **Heatmaps & Session Recording**: Understand user behavior visually
- **A/B Testing**: Test variations and optimize conversions
- **Tag Manager**: Manage tracking codes without code changes
- **Custom Reports**: Build reports tailored to your needs
- **100% Data Ownership**: Your data stays on your server

## Use Cases

- Websites needing privacy-compliant analytics
- E-commerce stores tracking conversions and revenue
- Marketing teams measuring campaign effectiveness
- Organizations requiring data sovereignty
- Publishers analyzing content performance
- Anyone wanting to replace Google Analytics

## Why Choose Matomo?

Matomo proves that powerful analytics and user privacy can coexist. You get all the insights you need while respecting your visitors and complying with privacy regulations.
