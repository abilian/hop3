# Cal.com

Cal.com is an open-source scheduling infrastructure that helps individuals and teams manage appointments and meetings. It's a powerful, self-hostable alternative to Calendly with advanced features for complex scheduling needs.

## Key Features

- **Smart Scheduling**: Automatically find the best meeting times based on availability
- **Calendar Integration**: Connect with Google Calendar, Outlook, and other calendar services
- **Team Scheduling**: Round-robin, collective, and managed event types for teams
- **Custom Booking Pages**: Branded scheduling pages that match your identity
- **Workflows & Automations**: Automated reminders, follow-ups, and custom actions
- **Video Conferencing**: Built-in integrations with Zoom, Google Meet, and more
- **Recurring Bookings**: Support for recurring appointments and subscriptions

## Use Cases

- Consultants and freelancers managing client appointments
- Sales teams scheduling demos and discovery calls
- Support teams offering customer success meetings
- Healthcare providers managing patient appointments
- Educators scheduling office hours and tutoring sessions

## Why Choose Cal.com?

Cal.com eliminates the back-and-forth of scheduling while giving you complete control over your data. Its open-source nature means you can customize it to fit your exact workflow needs.
