# LimeSurvey

LimeSurvey is the leading open-source survey software, enabling you to create professional online surveys for market research, customer feedback, academic studies, and more. It offers advanced features typically found only in expensive commercial solutions.

## Key Features

- **Powerful Survey Builder**: 30+ question types with conditional logic
- **Multi-Language Surveys**: Create surveys in multiple languages
- **Templates & Themes**: Customizable appearance to match your brand
- **Advanced Logic**: Branching, quotas, and complex expressions
- **Data Analysis**: Built-in statistics and export to Excel, SPSS, and more
- **Participant Management**: Token-based surveys with invitation tracking
- **Expression Manager**: Create dynamic surveys with calculated responses

## Use Cases

- Market research and customer satisfaction surveys
- Academic research and educational assessments
- Employee feedback and HR surveys
- Event registration and feedback collection
- Healthcare questionnaires and patient surveys
- Government and public sector consultations

## Why Choose LimeSurvey?

LimeSurvey provides enterprise-grade survey capabilities without the enterprise price tag. Its flexibility makes it suitable for everything from simple polls to complex research instruments.
