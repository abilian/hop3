# PeerTube

PeerTube is a decentralized video hosting platform that puts you in control of your content. Using peer-to-peer technology and federation, it provides a sustainable alternative to centralized video platforms like YouTube.

## Key Features

- **Decentralized**: Videos are distributed across federated instances
- **P2P Streaming**: Reduces server load through peer-to-peer distribution
- **Federation**: Connect with other PeerTube instances via ActivityPub
- **Live Streaming**: Built-in live streaming capabilities
- **Customizable**: Themes, plugins, and extensive configuration options
- **Transcoding**: Automatic video transcoding to multiple qualities
- **Playlists & Channels**: Organize content like traditional platforms
- **Moderation Tools**: Comprehensive tools for content moderation

## Use Cases

- Independent content creators seeking platform independence
- Organizations hosting internal video content
- Educational institutions sharing lectures and tutorials
- Communities building video sharing networks
- Media organizations distributing content
- Anyone escaping algorithmic content control

## Why Choose PeerTube?

PeerTube lets you own your video platform without the infrastructure costs of traditional hosting. Its federation model means your content can reach audiences across the network while you maintain control.
