# Rocket.Chat

Rocket.Chat is the leading open-source team communication platform, offering a secure and fully customizable alternative to Slack and Microsoft Teams. It provides messaging, video conferencing, and collaboration tools all in one place.

## Key Features

- **Real-Time Messaging**: Channels, direct messages, and threads
- **Video & Audio Calls**: Built-in video conferencing and voice calls
- **Federation**: Connect with users on other Rocket.Chat servers
- **Omnichannel**: Integrate customer communication from multiple channels
- **Bots & Integrations**: Connect with hundreds of external services
- **End-to-End Encryption**: Secure conversations with E2EE
- **White-Label**: Fully customizable branding
- **Mobile Apps**: Native iOS and Android applications

## Use Cases

- Team communication and collaboration
- Customer support and help desk
- Community building and engagement
- DevOps and incident management
- Education and virtual classrooms
- Healthcare communication (HIPAA compliant)

## Why Choose Rocket.Chat?

Rocket.Chat gives you Slack-like functionality with complete control. Self-hosting means your conversations stay private, and the open-source model ensures no vendor lock-in.
