# OpenProject

OpenProject is a powerful open-source project management solution that supports teams throughout the entire project lifecycle. From planning to execution to reporting, it provides tools for classic, agile, and hybrid project management approaches.

## Key Features

- **Work Packages**: Comprehensive task and issue management
- **Gantt Charts**: Interactive timeline planning and tracking
- **Agile Boards**: Scrum and Kanban boards for agile teams
- **Time & Cost Tracking**: Log time and manage budgets
- **Team Collaboration**: Forums, news, wikis, and activity feeds
- **Document Management**: Store and share project documents
- **Meeting Management**: Plan agendas and track minutes
- **Custom Fields & Workflows**: Adapt to your processes

## Use Cases

- Construction and engineering project management
- IT project portfolios and software development
- Marketing campaign planning
- Product development and roadmapping
- Research project coordination
- Any team needing structured project oversight

## Why Choose OpenProject?

OpenProject bridges the gap between simple task managers and complex enterprise tools. It's powerful enough for large organizations yet accessible to small teams.
