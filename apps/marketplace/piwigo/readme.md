# Piwigo

Piwigo is a professional photo gallery software for the web, designed to manage and share your photo collections. With over 20 years of development, it's a mature and reliable solution for photographers, organizations, and families.

## Key Features

- **Organize Photos**: Albums, tags, and smart albums for automatic organization
- **Batch Management**: Upload and edit thousands of photos efficiently
- **Privacy Controls**: Public, private, and password-protected galleries
- **User Management**: Multiple users with different permission levels
- **Responsive Design**: Beautiful viewing experience on all devices
- **Plugin System**: 350+ plugins for extended functionality
- **Themes**: Customizable appearance to match your style
- **External Sync**: Sync with Lightroom, digiKam, and mobile apps

## Use Cases

- Professional photographers showcasing portfolios
- Families sharing photos privately
- Organizations managing media libraries
- Schools and universities archiving events
- Museums and archives digitizing collections
- Anyone wanting a private alternative to cloud photo services

## Why Choose Piwigo?

Piwigo gives you complete control over your photos. No storage limits, no privacy concerns, no algorithm deciding what you see. Your photos, beautifully organized, on your terms.
