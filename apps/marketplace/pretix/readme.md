# Pretix

Pretix is a professional ticket shop application designed for conferences, festivals, concerts, exhibitions, and any event requiring sophisticated ticketing. It handles everything from simple ticket sales to complex multi-day events.

## Key Features

- **Flexible Ticketing**: Multiple ticket types, pricing tiers, and add-ons
- **Multi-Event Support**: Manage multiple events from one installation
- **Payment Processing**: Integrates with Stripe, PayPal, and many more
- **Check-In App**: Mobile apps for scanning tickets at entry
- **Seating Plans**: Reserved seating for venues
- **Waiting Lists**: Automatic notification when tickets become available
- **Vouchers & Discounts**: Promotional codes and special pricing
- **White-Label**: Fully customizable to match your brand

## Use Cases

- Conferences and tech events
- Concerts and music festivals
- Theater and performing arts venues
- Museums and exhibitions
- Sports events and competitions
- Workshops and training sessions

## Why Choose Pretix?

Pretix combines the polish of commercial ticketing platforms with the flexibility of open-source. It's trusted by major events worldwide and handles millions of tickets annually.
